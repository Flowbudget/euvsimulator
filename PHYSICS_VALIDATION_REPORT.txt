For sigma=0.8, NA=0.33, dm=1:
- x = π × 0.8 × 0.33 × 1 × 0.28125 = 0.23326
- TCC = 2 × J1(0.23326) / 0.23326 = 0.99321

DISCREPANCY RESOLVED:
The earlier diagnostic report incorrectly claimed x ≈ 2.33 and TCC ≈ 0.31.
The error was in manual calculation: the code uses wavelength_m/period_m = 13.5/48 = 0.28125
(not wavelength/pitch in different units). The actual x is 0.233, not 2.33.

The code formula EXACTLY matches standard Hopkins 1D TCC for circular source:
    TCC = 2 × J1(π × σ × NA × dm × λ/Λ) / (π × σ × NA × dm × λ/Λ)

No additional normalization or scaling found in TCC computation.
