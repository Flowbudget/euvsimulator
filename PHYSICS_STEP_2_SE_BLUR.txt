7. PHYSICS JUSTIFICATION

Secondary-electron blur is a RESIST effect, not an optical effect:

1. Optical imaging (mask -> aerial image):
   - Photons diffract at mask
   - Partially coherent interference (Hopkins TCC)
   - Pupil filtering (NA)
   - Defocus phase shifts
   - Result: Aerial image intensity I(x,y)

2. Resist exposure (aerial image -> acid):
   - Local dose = aerial image × incident dose
   - EUV photons absorbed in resist (Beer-Lambert)
   - Photoelectrons + Auger electrons generated
   - Electrons random-walk (nm scale) before generating acid
   - This IS the SE blur - happens INSIDE resist
   - Result: Blurred acid distribution

OLD code incorrectly placed SE blur in step 1 (optical).
NEW code correctly places SE blur in step 2 (resist exposure).

Verification: gaussian_se_blur applied once to dose_map in _cd_via_full_chem.
NILS computed on blurred dose_map (what resist sees).
dose_to_acid called with apply_blur=False.
